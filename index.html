About me

Driven by curiosity.
Grounded in making.

I’m Dheeraj Chauhan, a technology graduate, hands-on electronics developer and technical mentor. My knowledge has grown through building, testing, repairing and asking how things work.

Electronics magazines, technical books and practical experiments sparked a habit of learning through making. That curiosity connects my work on radio circuits and computer hardware with embedded products, connected systems and applied research.

I have founded a private limited company in India to take on professional product development and larger technical projects. I bring together circuit design, software, fabrication and system integration to turn ideas into working solutions.

I continue to study and experiment across electronics, automation and computing. For me, a useful idea becomes meaningful when it can be built, tested and understood.
